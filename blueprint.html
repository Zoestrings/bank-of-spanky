How Each Pillar Works

$SPANKY Liquidity Mechanics

A portion of mint funds pair with $SPANKY in a Raydium or Orca AMM pool to create trading depth. The reserve fund buys back floor NFTs during dips, maintaining price stability for long-term holders.

Content Production Model

An in-house team of editors and strategists handles daily posting across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, and X. Holders in the Vault Member+ tier get performance reports monthly.

Merch Quality Standards

All physical goods are produced through vetted premium manufacturers. Each item is inspected, tagged with the NFC/QR claim chip, and shipped in branded packaging designed in-house.

Revenue Share Structure

Legendary Vault Keepers participate in a direct revenue share from merch sales. Distribution is proportional to the number of 1-of-1s held and executed quarterly on-chain.
