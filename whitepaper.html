Section 01

Vision & Mission

The Bank of Spanky is an exclusive digital asset collective designed at the intersection of physical merchandise, blockchain technology, and community-driven media. It is not simply an NFT project — it is an infrastructure layer for a lifestyle brand backed by real-world goods and verified community access.

Our mission is to build the most tightly integrated merch-to-digital pipeline in Web3, where every physical product serves as a gateway to a permanent on-chain identity, community access, and financial participation in the brand's growth.

Core Principle: Every deposit builds the vault. Every holder becomes a stakeholder. Every piece of merch is a key.
Section 02

The Ecosystem

The Bank of Spanky ecosystem operates across four interconnected pillars:

  • NFT Collection A 333-piece genesis collection on Solana with tiered rarity and utility.
  • Merch Pipeline Physical goods that unlock companion NFT claims, creating a permanent merch-digital loop.
  • $SPANKY Token A community memecoin liquidity-paired using mint revenue.
  • Community Access Token-gated Discord, Telegram, and Spanky School channels for verified holders.

These four pillars reinforce each other: owning the NFT unlocks community access; buying merch creates a new NFT; holding the NFT earns $SPANKY; $SPANKY deepens ecosystem participation.

Section 04

Merch Pipeline

The Bank of Spanky Merch Pipeline is the core innovation of the project — a closed-loop system where physical product purchases generate on-chain assets automatically.

How It Works

  • Customer purchases an official item from ogspanky.com
  • Item ships with a physical NFC/QR authentication tag
  • Customer scans tag and connects a Solana wallet
  • Companion NFT mints gas-free into the customer's wallet
  • NFT grants community access and future drop priority

Each tag code is single-use and expires 90 days from purchase. Items manufactured through verified production partners include apparel, headwear, collector pins, and limited seasonal releases.

Section 05

Token Strategy

$SPANKY is the community memecoin of the Bank of Spanky ecosystem. It is not a security, utility token, or governance instrument — it is a cultural asset representing participation in Spanky World.

40% of genesis mint revenue is allocated to $SPANKY liquidity pool seeding on Raydium or Orca. Genesis NFT holders receive a proportional $SPANKY allocation at launch, with Legendary Vault Keepers receiving the highest allocation per token held.

Note: $SPANKY is a memecoin with no guaranteed value. Allocation is a community gesture, not an investment. Never invest more than you can afford to lose.

Section 07

Governance

Governance in the Bank of Spanky ecosystem operates through holder voting in the verified Discord #governance channel. Voting rights are weighted by tier:

  • OG Holders — 1 vote per NFT held
  • Vault Members — 3 votes per NFT held
  • Legendary Vault Keepers — 10 votes per 1-of-1 held

Votes are non-binding advisory signals unless a quorum of 50%+ of eligible holders participate, at which point the founding team treats the outcome as directionally binding.
